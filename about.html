About LogicBox Lab

We're passionate about creating digital products that make a difference

Our Story

LogicBox Lab was founded with a simple mission: to create digital products that are both beautiful and functional. We believe that great software should solve real problems while being a joy to use.

What started as a passion project building tools for the Cyprus community has grown into a full-service web design and development studio. Our flagship product, the Cyprus Tax Calculator, helps thousands of residents understand their tax obligations under the 2026 tax reform.

Our Mission

We're on a mission to democratize access to complex calculations and information. Whether it's understanding tax obligations, planning finances, or streamlining business processes, we build tools that empower users with clarity and confidence.
